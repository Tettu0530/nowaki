## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are nominated by the lead maintainer after sustained contributions (code, reviews,
triage, docs or RFCs). The steps are in
[GOVERNANCE.md § Becoming a maintainer](./GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer). If you would
like to work toward it, say so in [Discussions](https://github.com/Tettu0530/nowaki/discussions)
or on a pull request.
